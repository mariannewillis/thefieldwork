A booking sits in the first table until its day has been. The morning after, it moves itself down to the archive — for a workshop or a course that is the day printed on it, and for a one-to-one it is that session’s own hour. There is nothing to file.

Every offering carries its own refund period, set when you make it: fourteen days on Reading the Field, twenty-one on The Long Attention, twenty-eight on the Autumn Course, two on a follow-up. Each row is measured against its own, and says which.

Cancel is the everyday one. It releases the place, and while the booking is still inside its refund period it asks whether to send the money back as well. Past that period it cancels without a refund and says so plainly. Refund can also be used on its own, later, if you change your mind. Delete stays out of reach until a booking has been cancelled — it takes the record away altogether, and it cannot be undone.
