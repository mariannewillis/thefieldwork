Getting there

  • The room is on the ground floor, and it is step-free from the pavement to the chair you sit in.
  • There is an accessible toilet on the same floor.
  • There is parking on Fromefield, two minutes’ walk away.
  • It is eight minutes on foot from the market place, uphill on the way here.
  • The buses along Bath Road stop at the top of Fromefield.

What the day is

You arrive at ten. There is coffee, a circle of ten chairs, and nine other people who also did not quite know what to expect. We go round the circle once, first names only, and you can say as little as you like.

The morning is spent on attention — how to tell the difference between what you are sensing and what you are supplying. You work on your own hands first, then with the person next to you. Most people find they have been doing this for years without a name for it.

After lunch you work in pairs, and you swap halfway. You stay clothed, seated and at arm’s length throughout. Nobody touches you. You can stop at any point, and you do not have to say why.

No belief is asked of you. People arrive sceptical and leave sceptical, and that is a perfectly good way to spend a Saturday.

The shape of the day

  • 10:00 Doors, coffee, and somewhere to put your coat.
  • 10:30 The circle, and the morning’s work on attention.
  • 12:30 Lunch at the long table. An hour, and you can go outside.
  • 13:30 Working in pairs, with a swap in the middle.
  • 15:30 Back to the circle for whatever came up.
  • 16:00 We finish. There is tea for anyone who wants to stay.

What to bring

Almost nothing. There is nothing to buy on the day and nothing you need to have read.

  • Lunch, coffee and tea are included. Tell me what you cannot eat when you book.
  • Wear whatever you can sit comfortably in for six hours.
  • Bring a notebook if you are someone who writes things down. About half the room usually is.
  • Bring a jumper. The room is warm at eleven and cooler by four.

Before you come

When you book, a short note comes with your confirmation — two pages on what to expect, and one question to sit with beforehand. You do not have to do anything with it, and nobody asks about it on the day.
