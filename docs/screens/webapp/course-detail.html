Six evenings, in order

The six evenings

Each evening runs two hours, in the same room, with the same six people. Missing one is possible to arrange — ask before the course starts, not after.

Evening 1 · Sitting in the chair Tue 15 Sep

How the hour is built, and how this course is built from six of them. Introductions, then everyone takes a first turn.

Evening 2 · Giving the hour Tue 22 Sep

Working in pairs, both sides of the chair — what it is like to give the hour, not only receive it.

Evening 3 · The field, described Tue 29 Sep

What people describe when they talk about the field, and where the evidence for it currently stands.

Evening 4 · The longer sequence Tue 6 Oct

Worked slowly, start to finish, with the rest of the group watching and offering feedback afterwards.

Evening 5 · The questions so far Tue 13 Oct

Whatever the group has been sitting with since week one, worked through together, out loud.

Evening 6 · Without a script Tue 20 Oct

The last evening, worked without a script, and what happens once the six weeks are over.

Who this is for

Foundations is not a qualification and doesn't lead to one — Practitioner Pathway is the training course, and it runs separately. This one is for six people who want longer than an hour with the practice: the same chair, the same room, six weeks running. Most have had at least one one-to-one session already. That isn't required.
