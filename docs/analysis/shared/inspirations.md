## Why the references are not wellness sites

`competitors.md` measured this category's ceiling and it is low: **one bespoke
build in six** (Sahana Sound, an Astro bundle), everything else a Squarespace /
Wix / Elementor / WordPress template. It also measured that **5 of 6 sites are
near-white or cream with exactly one warm accent**, that the typographic default
is an ultralight high-contrast serif over cream, and that **0 of 6 homepages
contain the words _clothed_, _undress_, _touch_, _hands-off_ or _believe_**.

Sourcing inspiration from inside that set caps this project at "the nicest reiki
site". So every reference below comes from an adjacent world that has already
solved one of **this** project's actual problems:

| This project's problem                                                      | The world that already solved it               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Present light itself with dignity (the mark is an emanating field)          | Light art — Turrell, Eliasson, Flavin, Chichu  |
| Be night-side and cosmic without new-age kitsch                             | Observatories, astronomy imagery, dark cinema  |
| Hold stillness as a composition rather than as empty space                  | Japanese _ma_, quiet architecture, galleries   |
| Be intimate and frightening AND credible AND not clinical AND not overclaim | Palliative care, hospice, bereavement, therapy |
| Typeset real dates and real prices beautifully                              | Concert halls, cinematheques, retreat centres  |
| Answer an embarrassing question plainly, before it is asked                 | Public-health editorial (NHS, Wellcome)        |
| Express a seven-part ascent without a rainbow                               | Rothko, Albers, Turrell's dusk programme       |

## Mood Definition

**Primary Mood**: _Night-side, unhurried, quietly certain — a low-lit room, not a
spa brochure._ The success test is the client's own (brief §2): a visitor should
feel as though their aura has been lifted just by having been on the site.

**Keywords**: emanating · low-lit · unhurried · plainspoken · dignified ·
instrumented · warm-dark · specific · non-clinical · unembarrassed

**Avoid**: soft-focus · lavender · pastel-spiritual · breathless · clinical ·
pampering · promissory · preset · loud

## Mood Keyword Clusters — enough range to seed TEN divergent territories

Ten clusters, one per style slot. These are **territories, not restatements**:
the exclusivity axes below show which pairs cannot coexist in one direction, so a
downstream reader can confirm the ten are genuinely divergent rather than one mood
said ten ways.

### A — Observatory / Celestial Instrument

`night-side` `instrumented` `measured` `ephemeris` `coordinates` `tabular`
`precise` `cold-light-warm-heart` `alignment` `logbook`
Dated events read as **observations**, not as marketing. The four verbs
(clearing, charging, repairing, restructuring) read as instrument operations.

### B — Light as Material

`aperture` `threshold` `plane-of-colour` `emanation` `edge` `dwell`
`almost-nothing` `one-field-per-view` `saturation-without-image`
The light IS the ground. Imagery is subordinate to a colour field that occupies
the viewport. Nearest to the logo's own logic.

### C — Consulting Room / Quiet Clinic

`plain` `unadorned` `sentence-first` `generous-measure` `credible-because-quiet`
`no-hero-image` `black-on-paper` `one-accent` `answerable`
Text leads. The first viewport is a paragraph, not a photograph. Credibility is
bought by refusing decoration.

### D — Repertory Programme / Season Card

`dated` `typeset` `tabular-figures` `ink-on-paper` `listings-as-hero`
`timecode` `rules-and-columns` `printed-season` `legible-at-a-glance`
The products block is the hero, not an afterthought. A season card you could
pin to a fridge.

### E — Nocturne / Chamber

`low-light` `warm` `hushed` `close-crop` `candle-scale` `intimate` `interior`
`small-type-on-a-large-dark-field` `held`
The register of one room at night with one lamp on. Figurative and warm.

### F — Devotional Minimal / _Ma_

`negative-space` `one-object-per-view` `slow` `asymmetric` `the-pause-is-the-composition`
`subtractive` `unornamented` `interval`
Emptiness carries meaning. Removal is the design method.

### G — Field Notebook / Practitioner's Record

`hand` `annotation` `marginalia` `worn` `evidence` `plainspoken` `verbs`
`fifteen-years` `record-not-claim`
Authority from accumulated record rather than from assertion. The honest register
for a practitioner with no institutional logos to borrow.

### H — Warm Domestic Interior

`a-real-room` `armchair` `lamplight` `hospitality` `unposed` `non-institutional`
`kettle-on` `ordinary` `welcome`
Directly served by `aura-light-in-a-room` (a real domestic interior). The
anti-clinical territory.

### I — Editorial Broadsheet

`masthead` `columns` `drop-cap` `rules` `reading-as-the-primary-act` `byline`
`long-form` `stated-plainly` `correction-notice`
Beat 5 ("what this is not") becomes an editorial column rather than a disclaimer.

### J — Emanation / Gradient Field

`radial` `concentric` `dilating` `gold-to-magenta` `aurora-without-rainbow`
`the-mark-as-the-grid` `falloff` `bloom`
The logo's concentric geometry promoted to the layout system itself.

### Exclusivity axes (which clusters cannot share a direction)

| Axis         | Mutually exclusive groupings                                                                                                                   |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ground**   | Dark-ground: **A · B · E · J** — Light-ground: **C · D · G · I** — Either: **F · H**                                                           |
| **Imagery**  | Non-figurative light/field only: **B · F · J** — Figurative-warm people-in-rooms: **E · H** — Text-led, imagery subordinate: **C · D · G · I** |
| **Ornament** | Subtractive (remove to necessity): **C · F** — Additive-atmospheric (bloom, falloff): **E · J** — cannot be both                               |
| **Motion**   | Earns slow ambient motion: **B · J** — Forbids motion beyond state change: **C · D · G · I**                                                   |
| **Hero**     | Image-hero: **B · E · H · J** — Type-hero: **C · G · I** — **Listings-hero: D** (the only cluster where the products block leads)              |

Any two clusters that sit on opposite ends of ≥2 axes are guaranteed divergent.
A–J as listed cover both ends of all five axes.

## ANTI-MOOD — what this project must not feel like

Every entry is a **measured** finding from `competitors.md`, not an assertion,
with the reason it fails **this** client specifically.

| Cliché (measured)                                                                                                                                  | Why it fails The Field Work                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Soft-focus lavender / cream-with-one-warm-accent** (5 of 6 sites; accents `#E1CCBE`, `#AB8041`, `#BFAD99`)                                       | Brief §2 asks for night-side. Adopting the measured default forfeits the **only unoccupied territory in the category** — dark grounds are 1 of 6, and that one is a US bathhouse, not a competitor.                                                  |
| **Chakra rainbow chart / seven-colour band**                                                                                                       | Brief §20 names it the category cliché. It converts a content structure into a diagram, and a diagram asks the visitor to accept a system **before** being told what physically happens. Beat 1's job is the exact opposite.                         |
| **Cupped hands around a glowing orb**                                                                                                              | The project **owns** this image (`aura-hands-between`) and the inventory marks it reserve-only. It is the single most-reproduced composition in the category — using it as a hero makes a commissioned plate read as stock.                          |
| **Mandala / sacred-geometry watermark**                                                                                                            | Belief-first iconography. The Carrier's stated fear is "being asked to believe something" (§3). This is that fear rendered as ornament.                                                                                                              |
| **Crystals, candles, singing bowls, empty treatment rooms**                                                                                        | The measured imagery default. They signal the **modality**; the Carrier's question is **procedural** — what happens to my body. Props answer the wrong question.                                                                                     |
| **Spa-brochure serenity** (Blossom Reiki ships the literal "Spa and Salon" theme, sold for "spa, salons, beauty, girly, hair… parlor")             | Reads as pampering. Pampering is a purchase the Carrier is not making — they arrive carrying grief, burnout, or a fog no test explains.                                                                                                              |
| **Breathless transformation copy** ("heal your life"; Aura Quartz's _"Confident, Happy, Calm, Relaxed, Loved, Safe"_)                              | Violates principle 3 (honesty over persuasion) and is CAP/ASA-exposed under §14. The category's incumbent voice; the brief's "warm, unhurried, quietly certain" has **no incumbent**.                                                                |
| **Mechanism claims dressed in physiology** (Sahana: _"stimulates the parasympathetic nervous system"_, _"allows the immune system to regenerate"_) | Precisely the claim class the ASA requires clinical-trial substantiation for. Two upheld rulings on the record (Allan Sweeney 2011; Healing on the Streets – Bath 2013).                                                                             |
| **Enquiry-gated pricing as a premium signal** (Sahana, Aura Quartz)                                                                                | The Carrier persona names "a price that only appears after you make contact" as the thing that breaks their day (§6). It reads premium to the convinced and evasive to the sceptical — the wrong trade.                                              |
| **PP Editorial New Ultralight (or any ultralight didone-ish serif) over cream**                                                                    | Measured as the most-copied wellness typographic move of 2022–2026. Adopting it is now indistinguishable from adopting a preset. **Note the trap: the wordmark is Cormorant-family, so "just use Cormorant Light on cream" walks straight into it.** |
| **Condensed display face + one acid accent on a dark plum ground**                                                                                 | Othership's exact combination at `#372338`, which sits ~one step from this brief's working `#2B0E28`. A dark direction that also takes a condensed face reads as an Othership derivative. Take the courage, not the costume.                         |
| **Scroll-fade-in + testimonial carousel as the entire motion vocabulary**                                                                          | The measured motion default across the whole set ("no site in the set uses motion as meaning"), and a direct C-5 static-review hazard — content gated behind `opacity:0` is banned.                                                                  |
| **A third-party booking widget in a foreign register** (Blossom → Calendly; Re:Mind → Momence)                                                     | Breaks visually every time: different type, colour, radius, motion. Owning the slot picker is a **visible** quality difference, not an engineering preference.                                                                                       |

## Reference Designs

### Group 1 — Light as the subject, not the decoration

#### Inspiration 1: Roden Crater (James Turrell / Skystone Foundation)

- **Link**: http://rodencrater.com/
- **What to take**: **"Celestial Events" as a first-class navigation item** — a
  dated schedule of alignments, sitting at peer level with About and Spaces. Light
  itself is the programme. Map this straight onto the products block: workshops and
  course sessions presented as **dated events of the field**, not as a shop.
- **Relevance**: Solves "how do you give an emanating field top billing without it
  becoming wallpaper." The site is dark-ground and opens on moving light above the
  fold rather than on a mission statement — the inverse of the Squarespace order
  `competitors.md` measured. It is also the reference for Cluster A + B.
  <!-- Verified 200. Content extraction was thin (video-first, and the page title still leaks a WordPress default), so the claim here is deliberately limited to structure and ground, not to typographic craft. -->

#### Inspiration 2: Chichu Art Museum, Benesse Art Site Naoshima

- **Link**: https://benesse-artsite.jp/en/art/chichu.html
- **What to take**: The **"what you see depends on when you come"** framing — an
  underground museum lit only by daylight, so a Turrell and a Monet change with the
  hour and the season. Steal it as the honest reason a visitor should look at the
  dated programme rather than a static "book a session" button.
- **Relevance**: Ando + Turrell + Monet is the exact intersection of _ma_,
  light-as-material, and institutional credibility this brief needs (Clusters B + F).
  It is a serious, non-mystical presentation of the same subject matter.

#### Inspiration 3: Little Sun (Olafur Eliasson)

- **Link**: https://littlesun.org/
- **What to take**: Light as a **warm, useful, unembarrassed** object — the
  gold/amber emanation is the brand and it is never mystical. One radiating form,
  used at every scale, doing the work a logo usually needs a system to do.
- **Relevance**: Directly answers "how do we use a gold radial emanation without it
  reading as new-age." The brief's mark is a radiating field; this is that geometry
  operated by a designer who refuses kitsch. Cluster J.

#### Inspiration 4: Studio Olafur Eliasson

- **Link**: https://olafureliasson.net/
- **What to take**: The **archive-as-navigation** discipline — enormous quantity of
  work presented without a single decorative flourish, so the works carry all the
  colour. Useful counterweight for a project whose imagery is strong: let the six
  plates be the only chroma and keep the chrome achromatic.
- **Relevance**: The plates in `assets/images/` are commissioned and strong. The
  risk is a design that competes with them. This is the reference for chrome that
  gets out of the way.

#### Inspiration 5: Dia Art Foundation (Dan Flavin Art Institute)

- **Link**: https://www.diaart.org/visit/visit-our-locations-sites/dan-flavin-art-institute
- **What to take**: Flavin's premise — **fluorescent light installed as
  architecture, so the room becomes the artwork**. Translate to: the page ground is
  not a backdrop for the field, the ground **is** the field, and content sits inside
  it.
- **Relevance**: Cluster B's structural precedent. Dia is also the institutional
  register for presenting difficult non-representational work seriously.

### Group 2 — Night-side and cosmic without new-age kitsch

#### Inspiration 6: ESA/Webb

- **Link**: https://esawebb.org/
- **What to take**: **Astronomical imagery as primary content, each release stamped
  with its date and an identifier** (e.g. `weic2615`), never as background
  enhancement. The image is the entry point; the date is part of the object.
- **Relevance**: The honest lesson is inverted and therefore more useful — **Webb's
  own site is a LIGHT ground.** So the credibility does not come from darkness; it
  comes from treating the imagery as evidence with a date on it. A dark direction
  here must earn its darkness from the artwork (`#E9C87E` on `#2B0E28` = 10.89:1),
  not from cosmic mood.

#### Inspiration 7: ESO Photo Archive

- **Link**: https://www.eso.org/public/images/
- **What to take**: A **large grid of deep-field imagery on a dark ground with
  achromatic chrome** — the only saturated colour in the viewport belongs to the
  photographs. Exactly the discipline the six plates need.
- **Relevance**: Proof that "dark + cosmic" is an institutional register, not a
  spiritual one. Cluster A.

#### Inspiration 8: NASA Webb Space Telescope (STScI)

- **Link**: https://webbtelescope.org/
- **What to take**: The **caption contract** — every image carries what it is, when
  it was captured, and what you are actually looking at. Steal it for beat 2:
  describe the sensory experience with the same "here is what is happening"
  plainness rather than with adjectives.
- **Relevance**: Serious presentation of the invisible made visible — the literal
  subject of aura work — with zero mystical framing.

#### Inspiration 9: Griffith Observatory

- **Link**: https://griffithobservatory.org/
- **What to take**: A **public institution whose whole job is making the cosmic
  approachable to a first-time visitor who feels unqualified.** Note how it front-loads
  practical logistics (free, when to come, what you will see) over wonder.
- **Relevance**: The Carrier persona arrives feeling unqualified and half-expecting
  embarrassment. This is the same visitor, and this institution answers logistics
  before awe — the ordering the brief's Beat 1 requires.

### Group 3 — The stillness register (_ma_, quiet architecture, restraint)

#### Inspiration 10: John Pawson

- **Link**: https://www.johnpawson.com/
- **What to take**: **The interval as the composition.** Pawson's work and its
  presentation both treat the gap between elements as the designed thing. One image,
  enormous margin, no caption competing.
- **Relevance**: Cluster F's precedent, and the direct antidote to the measured
  category default of a full-bleed hero followed immediately by three-up cards.

#### Inspiration 11: Vitsœ

- **Link**: https://www.vitsoe.com/
- **What to take**: **Copy that argues against its own sale** — Vitsœ's long-standing
  position that you should buy less, better, and keep it for life. It is the most
  credible commercial voice on the web precisely because it declines to persuade.
- **Relevance**: **This is the model for Beat 5 ("what this is not").** Brief
  principle 3 says naming what the work is _not_ buys more trust than one more
  testimonial. Vitsœ has run that experiment for fifty years and it works.

#### Inspiration 12: Norm Architects

- **Link**: https://normcph.com/
- **What to take**: **Warm minimalism** — restraint that is not cold, achieved with
  material warmth and low-contrast tonality rather than with added colour.
- **Relevance**: Resolves §2's hardest constraint directly: "warmth without
  credibility fails as badly as credibility without warmth." Minimalism usually buys
  credibility by going cold. This does not.

#### Inspiration 13: Kinfolk

- **Link**: https://www.kinfolk.com/
- **What to take**: **Slowness as an editorial position** rather than as a visual
  effect — long measure, unhurried pacing, imagery that shows ordinary life rather
  than styled serenity.
- **Relevance**: Cluster H. Caution flagged deliberately: Kinfolk is close enough to
  the wellness preset that it should be taken for **pacing** only, never for palette
  or type.

### Group 4 — Credible-but-warm practitioner presentation (the closest functional analogue)

> These sites face **the same problem this brief faces**: an intimate, frightening
> subject; the need to be credible without being clinical; and a hard prohibition on
> overclaiming. This group is the most directly transferable in the whole document.

#### Inspiration 14: Mettle Health ★ strongest reference in this document

- **Link**: https://www.mettlehealth.com/ · about: https://www.mettlehealth.com/about
- **What to take**: **The refusal, stated first, as the opening line.** Verbatim
  from the homepage:

  > _"We can't change your situation, but we can help you live with it."_
  >
  > _"We don't offer easy answers because we know that for many of these
  > challenges, there are no simple fixes. Instead, we offer a place to sit with the
  > reality of your situation and navigate it together."_

  And the Open Wound named in **the visitor's own sentences**, verbatim:

  > _"Perhaps you will recognize yourself in one or more of the following
  > statements: 'I don't know where to start.' 'I don't know what we need.' 'I want
  > help making a plan.' 'I need someone who can help me sit with it all.' 'If I
  > talk to one more doctor, I might lose it.' 'I feel at such a loss. This is all
  > so hard.'"_

  Steal both moves: **lead with what you cannot do**, and **name the pain in
  quotation marks in the visitor's voice** rather than in the practitioner's.

- **Relevance**: A palliative-care consultancy (founded by BJ Miller) with the
  identical constraint set — cannot promise an outcome, must be credible, must be
  warm, sells a human conversation rather than a product. It converts the CAP/ASA
  constraint (§14) from a compliance tax into the strongest copy on the page. The
  narrative-spine `openWound` and the R-3 specificity gate both have their answer here.

#### Inspiration 15: Maggie's

- **Link**: https://www.maggies.org/ · centres: https://www.maggies.org/our-centres/
- **What to take**: **"Everyone's home of cancer care"** — the institution is framed
  as a _home_, not a facility, and the buildings (by Gehry, Hadid, Heatherwick,
  Pawson) enforce it: no reception desk, a kitchen table, a kettle. Verbatim:

  > _"If you or someone you love has cancer, we're here for you. We provide free
  > expert care and support from our centres across the UK and online."_

  The move: **second-person, immediate, and it states the terms (free, here, now)
  in the first sentence.** No mission statement precedes it.

- **Relevance**: The definitive proof that a frightening subject is best handled by
  architecture and hospitality rather than by clinical signalling. Cluster H's anchor,
  and the model for how `aura-light-in-a-room` (a real domestic interior) should be
  used — as a room you are being invited into.

#### Inspiration 16: Cruse Bereavement Support

- **Link**: https://www.cruse.org.uk/understanding-grief/
- **What to take**: **Understanding before service.** The primary navigation leads
  with _understanding grief_ — the visitor's state — not with the organisation's
  offerings. The catalogue comes later, once the visitor has been met.
- **Relevance**: The exact ordering of the seven beats. Beats 1–6 meet the person;
  the products block comes at position six-and-a-half. This is a UK charity that has
  validated that order at scale.

#### Inspiration 17: Marie Curie

- **Link**: https://www.mariecurie.org.uk/help
- **What to take**: **Plain nouns for hard things.** End-of-life care named directly,
  in ordinary words, with no euphemism and no softening imagery — which reads as more
  respectful, not less.
- **Relevance**: Beat 1 must say "you stay clothed, nobody touches you" in exactly
  this register. Euphemism here would reintroduce the embarrassment the beat exists
  to remove.

#### Inspiration 18: Sue Ryder

- **Link**: https://www.sueryder.org/how-we-can-help
- **What to take**: **"How we can help" as the top-level label** — capability framed
  from the visitor's need rather than from the service catalogue's structure.
- **Relevance**: A one-word fix available to the services/workshops/courses index
  pages, whose default framing is the owner's taxonomy rather than the visitor's need.

#### Inspiration 19: Modern Loss

- **Link**: https://modernloss.com/
- **What to take**: **Candour with humour and colour about grief** — proof that the
  register for a heavy subject does not have to be muted, and that warmth is not the
  same thing as softness.
- **Relevance**: Permission structure for Cluster I. The category's assumption that
  a serious subject requires a desaturated palette is exactly the assumption
  `competitors.md` measured and the brief rejects.

#### Inspiration 20: Two Chairs

- **Link**: https://www.twochairs.com/
- **What to take**: **The matching/intake explanation as a designed surface** — the
  site's core job is explaining what happens before you commit, and it gives that
  explanation real estate rather than an FAQ link.
- **Relevance**: `competitors.md` measured "the FAQ as a reassurance dumping-ground"
  across Blossom, Re:Mind and Othership. This is the same content promoted to a
  designed front-door surface — which is precisely Beat 1's structural move.

#### Inspiration 21: Alma

- **Link**: https://helloalma.com/
- **What to take**: **Practitioner-first presentation** — a real named human with a
  face and a specific description of how they work, before any modality explanation.
- **Relevance**: Beat 4 is "the practitioner enters — empathy first, credentials
  second." A sole operator cannot borrow authority the way Sahana borrows spa
  residencies and Nike logos; being specifically, recognisably human is the
  available alternative and this is the reference for it.

### Group 5 — Dated schedules typeset beautifully

> The products block is exactly this problem, and the arts have solved it. Note the
> **negative** finding at Inspiration 25 — it is as instructive as the positive ones.

#### Inspiration 22: Metrograph ★

- **Link**: https://metrograph.com/calendar/
- **What to take**: **The standalone timecode as a typographic anchor.** Date is a
  section header (`Saturday July 25`); showtimes — `12:00pm` · `1:25pm` · `8:10pm` —
  sit as independent, tappable objects beside the film block rather than buried in
  its description. Director / year / runtime / format run as a single small
  metadata line beneath the title.
- **Relevance**: The best available model for the **service free-slot picker** and
  for the products block simultaneously. It solves "scan by time" and "scan by
  event" in one layout, which is exactly the Returner persona's task ("scan upcoming
  dates" in under a minute) and the booking screen's task in one move. Cluster D's
  anchor.

#### Inspiration 23: Elbphilharmonie

- **Link**: https://www.elbphilharmonie.de/en/whats-on
- **What to take**: **The bold date–time pairing opening every entry** — `Sat, 15 Aug
2026` / `20:00` as the first thing in the card, with venue, artist and programme
  descending in weight beneath it. Availability is stated inline and honestly:
  _"Sold out"_ · _"Remaining tickets occasionally available at box office"_ ·
  _"Tickets from €29.50"_.
- **Relevance**: Brief §5 requires capacity to be shown **as information, never as a
  live seat count**. Elbphilharmonie's inline availability phrasing is the exact
  register for that — informative, hedged, honest. Also the model for showing a price
  on the listing, which half the measured category refuses to do.

#### Inspiration 24: Gaia House retreat programme

- **Link**: https://gaiahouse.co.uk/programme/ · context: https://gaiahouse.co.uk/retreats/
- **What to take**: **The data model, not the typography.** A representative row,
  verbatim: `26160 | Sep | Residential | A Path of Wisdom and Love | 12/09/26 |
17/09/26 | 5 | Jake Dartington & Gavin Milne | All | Spaces`. Fields:
  start date, end date, **number of nights**, teacher(s), suitability, availability,
  tiered pricing. Expectation-setting copy is plain and slightly forbidding:
  _"All of our retreats (except the Family Retreat) are held in silence."_
- **Relevance**: This is **the Course→Sessions shape**, already in production at a UK
  retreat centre — a multi-day dated span sold as one unit, with sessions inside it.
  It also demonstrates the failure mode to design against: presented as a raw sortable
  table, it is complete and unlovely. Take the completeness; typeset it like
  Inspiration 22.

#### Inspiration 25: Barbican — the instructive failure

- **Link**: https://www.barbican.org.uk/whats-on
- **What to take**: **What NOT to do.** Verified at fetch time: the event cards carry
  category tag, headline, description snippet and hero image — and **dates are largely
  absent from the cards themselves**, pushed into date-range filters ("Today", "Next 7
  days", "Next 30 days") and a separate calendar view. Discovery is optimised for
  theme; chronology is demoted.
- **Relevance**: A world-class arts institution reproducing the exact failure the
  Returner persona cannot tolerate. §15's booking-friction metric (landing → submitted
  request in <60s, ≤3 clicks) dies if the date is behind a filter. **Put the date on
  the card.**

#### Inspiration 26: Serpentine Galleries

- **Link**: https://www.serpentinegalleries.org/whats-on/
- **What to take**: **Exhibition run-spans typeset as first-class objects** — an event
  that occupies a range of dates rather than a point in time, given a legible
  begins/ends treatment.
- **Relevance**: A Course is a span; a Workshop is a point; a Service is a
  slot. Three different temporal shapes need three legible treatments **at equal
  billing** (§4). This is the span case.

#### Inspiration 27: Wigmore Hall

- **Link**: https://www.wigmore-hall.org.uk/whats-on
- **What to take**: A chamber-scale UK venue publishing a dense recital calendar with
  prices, at a scale closer to this practice than an opera house's.
- **Relevance**: The nearest UK peer in ambition — small venue, frequent dated events,
  a loyal returning audience.

## The seven-chakra arc WITHOUT the rainbow

Brief §11 fixes the seven beats in a **fixed ascending order** root→crown, and §20
records the strong default that **at most one true chakra hue appears per beat as a
small accent, never as a full band.** `competitors.md` confirms the rainbow chart as
the measured category cliché. So the ascent must be carried by something other than
hue rotation. This is a real design problem and it has real precedents.

### Four non-rainbow mechanics for an ascending seven-part arc

| Mechanic                         | How it reads as an ascent                                                      | Where the brief already supports it                                                               |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Luminance ascent**             | Each beat's ground lifts one measured step; beat 7 is the brightest point      | §11 states beat 7 is literally "at the brightest point of the page"                               |
| **Colour-temperature drift**     | Deep plum-blue at root → warm gold at crown, with no hue _jump_ anywhere       | The artwork's own gradient runs gold → coral → rose → magenta; a temperature axis, not a spectrum |
| **Scale / aperture progression** | The emanation's radius grows beat to beat; type scale opens as the page climbs | The mark is concentric light running outward from a centre                                        |
| **Density progression**          | Root is dense and close-set; crown is spacious and open                        | Beat 1 answers a fear (dense, factual); beat 7 is a single call to action (open)                  |

Combine **two** of these, never four. Using all four at once produces a page that
lurches rather than climbs.

### Inspiration 36: Rothko Chapel ★ (arc reference)

- **Link**: https://rothkochapel.org/ · visit: https://rothkochapel.org/visit/
- **What to take**: **Fourteen canvases in one near-black chromatic family, read as a
  sequence.** The progression is carried entirely by value, edge quality and
  saturation — there is no hue rotation and no rainbow, and the sequence is
  unmistakable anyway.
- **Relevance**: The direct proof-of-concept for a seven-part ascent inside a single
  plum family. Fourteen is more than seven; if fourteen can hold a sequence without
  changing hue, seven can. This is the reference that makes the anti-rainbow default
  in §20 achievable rather than merely prohibitive.

### Inspiration 37: Josef & Anni Albers Foundation — _Homage to the Square_ (arc reference)

- **Link**: https://www.albersfoundation.org/
- **What to take**: **Progression created by adjacency, not by spectrum.** In the
  _Homage_ series the perceived advance/recede comes from how each square sits against
  its neighbour; isolate any single swatch and the effect vanishes. Albers'
  _Interaction of Color_ is the written method.
- **Relevance**: Operationally: **each beat's ground shifts one measured step against
  the beat immediately before it.** Nobody perceives a rainbow because there isn't
  one — but everybody feels the climb, because the climb lives in the transitions.
  This is also the honest answer to the accessibility constraint: adjacency-driven
  steps can be tuned to keep every text/ground pair above the AA floor, which a hue
  rotation through the artwork's mid-stops cannot (`gold` fails at 1.47:1 on blush).
  <!-- Verified 200 at the foundation root. Deeper /artists/josef-albers/ paths returned HTTP 500 at analyze time — link the root, not a sub-page. -->

### Inspiration 38: Turrell's Skyspace dusk programme, via Roden Crater (arc reference)

- **Link**: http://rodencrater.com/ (see "Celestial Events")
- **What to take**: A Skyspace runs a **timed sequence at dusk** in which the aperture
  of sky appears to shift through colour temperature while the surrounding light is
  raised in slow steps. The perceived transformation is enormous; the actual hue
  travel is small.
- **Relevance**: The single best precedent for "the page ends in light" without a
  chakra band. It also validates the aperture mechanic — the concentric field
  **opening** beat by beat, which is the brief's own mark performing the ascent.

### Inspiration 39: Tala (arc reference, engineering analogue)

- **Link**: https://tala.co.uk/pages/our-story · products: https://tala.co.uk/collections/all
- **What to take**: **Warm-dim as a designed curve** — lighting engineered so that
  colour temperature drops as brightness drops, imitating a filament. The relationship
  between brightness and warmth is a designed function, not a side effect.
- **Relevance**: A specifiable curve for the luminance × temperature ascent above, from
  a lighting company that had to solve it in physics. Gives the ascent a defensible
  spec rather than seven hand-picked hex values.

## Micro-interactions & Animation References

**Hard constraint first.** §2 requires every motion to have a
`prefers-reduced-motion` fallback that leaves the composition whole, and C-5
bans content gated behind `opacity:0` + IntersectionObserver. The measured
category motion default is "a scroll fade-in and a testimonial carousel, and
nothing else" — reproducing it is both slop and a static-review failure. Motion
here must be **progressive enhancement on already-visible content**.

## Typography Mood Examples

Not the final picks — `styles.md` decides. All families below are Google Fonts
(full family names given) so the UI Designer can source them; the analyst
recommends only and downloads nothing.

**The trap to name explicitly.** The wordmark is set in a **Cormorant-family**
display face (per `assets/brand/README.md`) — which is a fact about the logo, not
a mandate for the site. `competitors.md` measured that an ultralight
high-contrast serif over cream is _the_ wellness preset (PP Editorial New
Ultralight, Marcellus, Raleway). **Cormorant Light on ivory walks straight into
that preset while feeling brand-native.** If a direction uses Cormorant, it must
earn it — at weight, at scale, or on a dark ground — not at Light on cream.

| Mood / cluster                 | Font Pairing (Google Fonts names)                         | Why it fits — and what it avoids                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Observatory / instrumented (A) | **Fraunces** display + **IBM Plex Sans** body             | Plex ships true tabular figures for dates, times and prices; Fraunces' optical-size axis gives warmth at display size |
| Light as material (B)          | **Instrument Serif** display + **Inter** body             | Very few glyphs on screen, each large; Inter's tabular figures cover the schedule                                     |

| Nocturne (E)                   | **Cormorant Garamond** at 600+ on dark + **Figtree** body | Brand-native, but at weight and on a dark ground — the opposite of the preset                                         |
| Devotional minimal / _ma_ (F)  | **Spectral** display + **Inter** body, very wide measure  | Spectral holds long measure and heavy leading; the interval carries the register                                      |
| Field notebook (G)             | **Literata** display + **Public Sans** body               | Reading-first, record-like, no glamour                                                                                |
| Warm domestic (H)              | **Fraunces** display + **Figtree** body                   | Both warm and round; no clinical edge                                                                                 |
| Editorial broadsheet (I)       | **Libre Caslon Text** display + **Source Sans 3** body    | Masthead register for Beat 5 as an editorial column                                                                   |
| Emanation (J)                  | **Syne** or **Bricolage Grotesque** display + **Inter**   | The only cluster where a distinctive display face is safe, because the layout is already non-standard                 |

**Non-negotiables from §2**: body text ≥16px; audience skews 35–65 with reading
glasses elsewhere in the house, so the practical body floor is 17–18px on the
public site; touch targets ≥44px; every text/ground pair verified by **computed**
contrast, because this palette's mid-stops fail in ways invisible to a designer who
already knows what the text says.
